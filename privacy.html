Privacy Policy

ItsDrDan Shorts Uploader · Last updated October 31, 2026

Who uses this app

ItsDrDan Shorts Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner, ItsDrDan (Dr. Dan), to upload his own videos as drafts to his own TikTok account. It has no other users.

What information it accesses

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives only what the approved permissions allow:

  • user.info.basic – the account’s basic profile (display name and avatar), used to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • video.upload – permission to send videos to the account’s TikTok inbox as drafts.

How it is stored

The sign-in token TikTok provides is stored only on the owner’s own computer. The app has no server and no database, and it does not collect, sell, or share any personal information with anyone.

Students and other viewers

This app does not collect any information about viewers of the videos or about students who use ItsDrDan’s study games.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions, which also invalidates the stored token.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the ItsDrDan YouTube channel: youtube.com/@itsdrdan.
