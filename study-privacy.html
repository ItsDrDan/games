Privacy: the Study Hub and study games

Dr. Dan’s Chemistry Study Hub (itsdrdan.github.io) · Last updated November 13, 2026

The short version
  • You never make an account. The hub does not ask for your name, email, or student ID to study.
  • Games send anonymous practice stats (which topic, right or wrong) so Dr. Dan can see what students find hardest.
  • Your progress (streak, checklists, recent chapters) is saved only in your own browser.
  • Your name is sent only if you type it into a feedback form.
  • There are no ads, and nothing is sold.

Anonymous practice stats

When you answer a question in a game or in Daily 5, the page sends one small record to a spreadsheet that Dr. Dan owns (a Google Apps Script). Each record holds:

The game, course, and modeStoichiometry, CHEM 3A, Practice
The topic or chapterCh 11 Stoichiometry
Whether the first answer was right, and the kind of slip if a mistake-catcher recognized onewrong, “forgot to convert °C to K”
For multiple-choice questions: the question’s ID, which choice was picked, and which was correctchoice 2 of 4
A random code for this browser tabk3x9q2ab, new every time you open a tab
How you arrived, as one wordcanvas, hub, embed, or direct

It does not hold your name, email, student ID, grade, or anything you type as an answer. The random code is not connected to you or to Canvas, so the records cannot be matched to a person or used for a grade.

To turn it off: switch on “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control” in your browser. The games check for both and send nothing when either is on.

What stays on your device

These are saved in your browser’s local storage, never sent anywhere, and are different on each device you use:

  • your Daily 5 streak, today’s five questions, and which chapters you missed recently
  • the chapters and games you opened last (for “Pick up where you left off”)
  • boxes you ticked on exam and skill-check checklists
  • the course you picked last, and a saved copy of the game list so the page loads faster
  • progress inside each game

To erase it, use “Clear this list” on the home page for recent items, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings. Inside Canvas, some browsers keep this storage separate from the same site opened in its own tab.

Feedback forms

The “Rate this game” button and the CHEM 3B extra-credit feedback page are the only places a name can be sent, and only when you type one. They send your star rating and comment, your course and professor, and your name if you gave it. This goes to Dr. Dan, and for extra credit to your instructor. It is used to give credit and to improve the games. The form remembers your name and professor on your device so you do not have to retype them.

Other services the pages use

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub sees your IP address when a page loads.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface.
  • YouTube supplies video thumbnails. A video player loads only when you press play, using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced (“nocookie”) player.
  • LibreTexts and OpenStax open in a new tab when you follow a textbook link; their own policies apply there.

Students under 13

The hub is written for college students and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
